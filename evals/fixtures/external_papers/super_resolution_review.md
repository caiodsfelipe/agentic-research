# Does Super-Resolution Help Lesion Detection? (fictional paper, evaluation fixture)

Abstract. We review super-resolution preprocessing for endoscopic lesion detection across four datasets.

Results. Super-resolution gave no consistent detection gain (between -1 and +1 F1 points) and increased inference cost. Gains reported in earlier work disappeared under patient-level splits.

Conclusion. Super-resolution preprocessing is not a reliable way to improve detection of small or flat lesions.
