# ColonSet-A dataset notes (fictional)

ColonSet-A contains 12,000 annotated frames from 140 patients. Only 8% of the annotated polyps are flat (sessile); the rest are pedunculated or protruding.

The train/validation/test split is random by frame (80/10/10). Frames from the same patient and even the same video appear in both the training and the test set.

Annotations are bounding boxes drawn by a single annotator; no inter-annotator agreement was measured.
